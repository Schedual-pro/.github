## The problem we solve

Building a school's timetable is one of the hardest planning problems a school faces each year. Student subject choices, Higher and Standard Level combinations, shared classes, teacher availability, room capacity and curriculum rules all have to fit together at once — and a single change can ripple across the whole week. Many schools still do this by hand, or with tools that were never built for international curricula, losing weeks of a coordinator's time every year.

Schedual turns that work into something a school can do in an afternoon, and adjust with confidence.

## How it works

Schedual is built around a **constraint-optimisation engine**. Instead of placing lessons one by one, a school describes its reality — its curricula, cohorts, subject choices, staff, rooms and the rules that must hold — and Schedual searches for a timetable that satisfies all of it at once.

1. **Describe the school** — import or enter cohorts, subjects, student choices, teachers and rooms.
2. **Set the rules** — the hard ones that must never break (clashes, availability, capacity) and the softer preferences to optimise (compact days, balanced teacher load, a good spread of subjects).
3. **Generate** — a complete timetable where every hard rule is guaranteed satisfied, with the rest optimised.
4. **Refine** — adjust, re-run and compare versions, without reshuffling what already works.

Hard constraints are guaranteed before a result is ever returned; everything else is optimised around them. When a timetable genuinely cannot be built, Schedual explains *why* instead of silently dropping lessons.

## Built for international schools

Schedual models the curricula these schools actually run, not a generic grid:

- **IB continuum** — PYP, MYP and the Diploma Programme (HL/SL combinations, shared lessons, core sessions)
- **British** — GCSE, IGCSE and A-Level option blocks
- **French** — the Baccalauréat, with its specialities and options
- **American** — the US high-school model, including Advanced Placement

A single school can run more than one of these side by side.

## More than a timetable

- **Clash-free schedules** for the whole school, generated and refined in minutes
- **Student and teacher portals**, plus iOS and Android apps, so everyone sees their own up-to-date timetable
- **Cover and substitution** management for when staff are away
- **Exports** to PDF, Excel, CSV, iCal, OneRoster and Pronote, so Schedual fits the systems a school already uses

## Where your data lives

- Hosted in the **EU, on our own servers in France**
- Covered by a signed **GDPR (Article 28) data-processing agreement**
- **No model is trained** on pupil or staff data, and there are **no third-party trackers**
